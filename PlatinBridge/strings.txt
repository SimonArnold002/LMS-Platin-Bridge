PLUGIN_PLATIN_BRIDGE
	EN	Platin Hub Bridge

PLUGIN_PLATIN_BRIDGE_DESC
	EN	Presents the Platin Stereo Hub HT as an ordinary Lyrion player over UPnP. It offers Lyrion the formats the Hub plays and passes the audio through untouched, so DSP, upsampling and ReplayGain are left to SqueezeDSP. The player's power button switches the Hub on and off, and the Hub's own remote and power are followed in Lyrion. Bespoke to one renderer: it is not a general UPnP bridge.

PLUGIN_PLATIN_STATUS
	EN	Renderer

PLUGIN_PLATIN_STATUS_DESC
	EN	What the Hub reported about itself when it was last contacted. Nothing here needs setting: the Hub is found on the network, and the audio is shaped by Lyrion's own transcoding and SqueezeDSP.

PLUGIN_PLATIN_NOTFOUND
	EN	No Platin Hub has answered yet. Discovery repeats, so this fills in once the Hub is reachable on the network.
